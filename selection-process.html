The selection standard

Performance
creates the evidence.

A name, recommendation or social following cannot move a player forward. Every decision must be supported by observed performance and recorded evaluation.

Consistent

The same assessment framework is applied across official trials.

Multi-stage

No single drill or moment determines the complete decision.

Reviewed

Recorded evidence supports shortlisting and player-pool decisions.

Selection architecture

Nine stages.
One earned place.

The pathway separates eligibility, assessment, shortlisting and team selection so every status has a clear meaning.

01

Application verification

Identity, eligibility, role and submitted details are checked before a trial place is confirmed.

02

Baseline assessment

Core movement, coordination and fundamental cricket readiness are observed.

03

Role assessment

Players complete structured tests specific to their declared primary role.

04

Competitive scenarios

Skills are tested through game situations that require immediate decisions.

05

Evaluator review

Recorded scores and observations are checked across the complete assessment.

06

Shortlist decision

Players meeting the required standard move to the next official stage.

07

Advanced assessment

Shortlisted players face deeper role, match and pressure evaluation where required.

08

Qualified player pool

Approved players become eligible for the official team-selection mechanism.

09

Team selection

Teams select eligible players through the final approved Season 1 mechanism.

Reaching one stage does not guarantee progression to the next. Every official status is communicated separately.

The evaluation model

More than
raw skill.

SATAACK 50 requires players who can create impact inside a short, high-pressure contest.

01

Role execution

How effectively you perform the responsibilities of your cricket role.

02

50-ball impact

How quickly and meaningfully your contribution changes the contest.

03

Game intelligence

Awareness, decision-making, adaptability and situational understanding.

04

Pressure response

Execution, composure and intent when time and opportunity are limited.

05

Consistency

Repeatable quality across drills, scenarios and evaluator observations.

06

Fielding value

Movement, catching, throwing, awareness and energy without the ball.

07

Athletic readiness

Mobility, balance, acceleration and readiness for the league environment.

Role-specific evaluation

Your role.
Your evidence.

01Contact quality

Timing, control and the ability to access scoring areas.

02Scoring intent

Boundary options, rotation and productive use of limited balls.

03Situation response

Shot selection, adaptability and decision-making under pressure.

04Repeatability

Consistency across multiple deliveries and changing conditions.

The final assessment framework, criteria and any weightings will be approved and published before official evaluation begins.

Evaluation bands

A clear language
for performance.

Scores are interpreted in context—by role, session conditions and the evidence collected across the complete trial.

01Exceptional

Performance consistently demonstrates a high-impact standard for the role.

02Strong

Clear role quality with credible evidence of readiness to progress.

03Competitive

Relevant ability is visible, with some areas requiring further review.

04Developing

Promising elements are present but the current standard is not yet complete.

05Below requirement

The evidence recorded at this trial does not meet the progression standard.

Consistency across sessions and cities

Evaluation data may be reviewed and normalised to account for approved differences in conditions, session structure and assessment context. This does not replace evaluator judgment; it helps keep comparisons responsible.

Records, corrections and integrity

Questions have
an official route.

Official communication

Status updates are issued through the participant account and registered contact details.

Record correction

Factual errors in identity, attendance or published records can be submitted for review.

Integrity report

Suspected impersonation, unofficial payments or process interference should be reported immediately.

Important: A correction request reviews record accuracy. It is not a route to negotiate evaluator judgment or demand selection.

Selection questions

Clear before
you compete.

View all FAQs
Does one strong drill guarantee shortlisting?

No. Decisions are based on the evidence collected across the relevant assessment stages.

Are all roles scored in the same way?

No. Common qualities may be observed across every player, but technical evaluation is specific to the responsibilities of each role.

Will individual evaluator notes be public?

No. Private evaluation records remain protected unless a specific item is approved for participant release or public publication.

Can a player be asked to complete another assessment?

Yes. Further review may be used when selectors require additional evidence before making a progression decision.

Does entering the player pool guarantee a team place?

No. It makes the player eligible for consideration. Final selection depends on the approved team-selection process and squad requirements.
