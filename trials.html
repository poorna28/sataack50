Official assessment

Not just
a net session.

A SATAACK 50 trial is a structured assessment designed to identify players for the Season 1 selection pathway.

Participants demonstrate role-specific skills through practical exercises, competitive scenarios and match-relevant assessments. The focus is not only raw ability. Selectors also look for decision-making, pressure handling and the potential to influence a 50-ball contest.

Explore the selection process

Role-specific skill

Batting, bowling, all-round or wicketkeeping ability.

Competitive execution

How effectively you perform when time and options are limited.

50-ball impact

The instinct and intelligence to change a fast-moving game.

Who can register?

Bring your
best role.

Your background may be tennis-ball cricket, box cricket, academy, college, corporate or another competitive playing environment.

01

Batters

Timing, intent, power, rotation and situational awareness.

02

Bowlers

Accuracy, control, variation and wicket-taking ability.

03

All-rounders

The ability to influence the contest in more than one way.

04

Wicketkeepers

Glovework, movement, reactions, awareness and communication.

Men & Women: 18-30 years.
Valid proof of age and required identification/documentation are mandatory.
Registration does not guarantee a trial.
No walk-in trials will be permitted.
Only players receiving official SATAACK 50™ confirmation will be admitted to the designated trial.

Trial questions

Before you
step in.

View all trial FAQs
Does registering guarantee a trial place?

Your place is confirmed only after all required registration steps are complete and you receive an official confirmation.

Does attending guarantee selection?

No. Trial participation gives you the opportunity to be evaluated. Progress depends on performance, eligibility, team requirements and the official selection process.

Can tennis-ball and box-cricket players register?

Yes, provided you meet the eligibility requirements shown for the selected trial.

Is there a registration fee?

Any applicable fee will be displayed clearly before you submit or pay for a registration.

How will I receive my result?

Your trial status and applicable next steps will be communicated through your participant account, registered mobile number or email address.
