Cricket culture

The grounds
that raised
the game.

Before the floodlights and organised competition, there were open grounds, narrow streets, terrace walls and improvised boundaries.

A taped tennis ball. A borrowed bat. Bricks for wickets. Rules negotiated before the first delivery. These are not merely places where cricket is played. They are where players learn confidence, improvisation, competition and belonging.
