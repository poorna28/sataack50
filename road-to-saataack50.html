Official player pathway

Eight steps.
One earned place.

Every stage has a defined purpose, from registration through final squad selection.

  1. 01

    REGISTER

    Complete the official SATAACK 50™ player registration.

  2. 02

    PRELIMINARY SCREENING

    Submitted player information, cricketing background and supporting credentials are reviewed.

  3. 03

    TRIAL INVITATION

    Eligible shortlisted players receive official confirmation of their trial date and venue.

  4. 04

    ATTEND THE TRIAL

    Players undergo structured sporting evaluation.

  5. 05

    PROFESSIONAL EVALUATION

    Selectors assess batting, bowling, fielding, wicketkeeping, fitness, game awareness and role suitability, as applicable.

  6. 06

    PLAYER RANKING

    Trial performance and verified cricketing information contribute to the player's evaluation.

  7. 07

    PLAYER POOL

    Qualified players enter the SATAACK 50™ approved player pool.

  8. 08

    SQUAD SELECTION

    Final squad selection is undertaken in accordance with league requirements and team balance.

Eligibility

Men & Women: 18-30 years.

Valid proof of age and required identification/documentation are mandatory.

Registration does not guarantee a trial.

No walk-in trials will be permitted.

Only players receiving official SATAACK 50™ confirmation will be admitted to the designated trial.

Journey questions

Clear before
you begin.

View all FAQs
Does registering guarantee a trial place?

No. The application must satisfy the official eligibility and verification requirements before the trial place is confirmed.

Does attending a trial guarantee evaluation or shortlisting?

Attendance records participation. Progress depends on completing the required assessments and meeting the relevant standard.

Does a high trial score guarantee shortlisting?

No. Decisions may consider the complete role-specific assessment, competitive situations, consistency and evaluator review.

Does entering the player pool guarantee selection?

No. The qualified pool establishes eligibility for team consideration. Teams make final decisions through the approved selection mechanism.

Where will official results appear?

Updates will be issued through the participant account, registered contact details and official SATAACK 50 channels.
