THE SATAACK 50™ FORMAT

10 OVERS. 5 BALLS AN OVER.
50 BALLS AN INNINGS.

Every team receives one innings of 50 legal deliveries.

10 Overs x 5 Balls = 50 Balls.

A standard match therefore consists of Team A - 50 Balls and Team B - 50 Balls: 100 scheduled legal deliveries of competitive action.

HOW A MATCH WORKS

From toss
to result.

Toss > First Innings > 10 Overs / 50 Balls > Innings Break > Second Innings > Result

The first team sets the target. The second team receives up to 50 legal deliveries to chase it. The team with the higher total wins, subject to the official playing conditions.

  1. 01

    Match Preparation

    Teams confirm their playing line-ups, roles and match readiness under the competition regulations.

  2. 02

    The Toss

    The winning captain chooses whether to bat or field first, establishing the opening tactical challenge.

  3. 03

    First Innings

    The first batting side receives fifty balls to set the strongest possible total.

    Set the score
  4. 04

    Innings Break

    The first-innings score becomes the target. Both teams reset and prepare for the chase.

  5. 05

    The Chase

    The second team receives up to fifty balls to reach the target.

    Chase the target
  6. 06

    The Result

    Reach the target to win the chase. Prevent it to win the defence. Ties follow the official regulations.

WHY 50 BALLS?

Long enough
to build.
Short enough
to stay alive.

Fifty balls create urgency without eliminating cricketing strategy. Batters must balance aggression with wicket preservation. Bowlers have limited opportunities and every delivery has tactical importance. Captains must react quickly and fielders can influence the result with every run saved.

What the format changes

Every role
under pressure.

Impact can come from scoring, control, wickets, fielding, partnerships, awareness or leadership.

Batters

Start ready.

Find scoring options quickly, rotate the strike and balance boundary intent with wicket value.

  • Score without wasting deliveries
  • Read fields and match-ups
  • Build partnerships under pressure
Bowlers

Give every ball a purpose.

Combine variation with control and make limited opportunities difficult to score from.

  • Change pace, line and length
  • Create wickets with control
  • Execute under scoring pressure
Fielders

Turn effort into runs saved.

A catch, stop or run-out can directly change the target and the result.

  • Convert catching chances
  • Prevent avoidable boundaries
  • Maintain complete intensity
Wicketkeepers

See it early.

Connect bowler, field and captain through sharp collection, anticipation and communication.

  • Support stumpings and run-outs
  • Track batter movement
  • Sustain fielding tempo
Captains

Decide earlier.

Recognise momentum and adjust match-ups, resources and fields before the chance disappears.

  • Read conditions quickly
  • Manage bowling resources
  • React before the damage

Format questions

The essentials.
Answered.

Details beyond these fundamentals will be published in the official competition regulations.

Is SATAACK 50 played with a tennis ball?

Yes. SATAACK 50 is being developed as a competitive tennis-ball cricket league.

How many balls does each team receive?

Each team receives one innings of 50 legal deliveries.

Does each team bat only once?

Yes. Each team has one innings in the standard match structure.

How does a team win?

The first team sets a total. The second attempts to reach it within its available deliveries. The higher score wins.

What happens if the scores are tied?

The tie-break procedure will be defined in the official Rules and Regulations.

How are the 50 deliveries organised?

Each innings consists of 10 overs, with 5 legal deliveries in every over. Field restrictions and bowling allocations follow the official playing rules.

Is the format only for aggressive players?

No. Impact can come through scoring, control, wickets, fielding, partnerships, awareness or decision-making.
