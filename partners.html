Why Season 1?

Enter at
the beginning.

Founding partners do more than appear around the competition. They become part of its origin story.

  • A distinctive association with a new Indian sports property
  • Visibility across the player journey, not only match days
  • Access to trials, teams, original content and competition
  • Space to build category-relevant activations
  • A partnership shaped around real business objectives
  • Long-term value as the league expands across cities and seasons

Evidence before promises. Pre-season projections will be identified as projections. Verified participation, reach and engagement will be reported as it becomes available.

Partnership questions

Before we
begin.

Can we partner with a particular city or team?

Yes. City, team and trial-specific opportunities may be available, subject to category rights and commercial approval.

Are Season 1 founding partnerships available?

Selected founding categories remain open. Availability depends on the requested rights, category and existing commitments.

Can a partner contribute services or infrastructure?

Yes. Strategic value-in-kind partnerships may be considered where the contribution directly supports league delivery and has an agreed commercial value.

Can local and regional businesses participate?

Yes. National, regional and city-level opportunities can be designed around the partner’s objective and available inventory.

Are audience and reach figures guaranteed?

No. Pre-season projections will be clearly identified. Verified reach, participation and engagement will be reported as it becomes available.

Does an enquiry reserve a category?

No. A category is reserved only after commercial approval and completion of the required agreement.
