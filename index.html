What Happens After You Register?

Once you register, your playing credentials and performance information will be scrutinised and assessed by the SATAACK 50 team. Based on the evaluation, selected players will be ranked and shortlisted for the next stage.

Only shortlisted players will receive an official confirmation by email, specifying their trial date, time and venue.

Important: No Walk-In Trials shall be considered at all.

Registration does not guarantee eligibility or admission to the trials.

Trials are strictly by official invitation/confirmation only.

If you are shortlisted, you will be contacted through the official communication channel.

The player pathway

From trials
to the league.

Eligibility Criteria:
Open to boys and girls aged between 18 to 30 years. Age will be determined as of the cut-off date, 1 January 2026.

Registration alone does not mean you are eligible for the trials. The team will scrutinise and audit the performances and give an official email confirmation with trials date and venue. Any walk-in trials shall not be considered at all.

01

Register

Enter the official SATAACK 50 trial pool. Walk-in trials not accepted.

02

Trials

Show your skills under structured evaluation. Age proof is must to confirm 18 to 30 years bracket for boys and girls.

03

Get evaluated

Batting, bowling, fielding, awareness and role fit. Evaluation will be done on the basis of past performances.

04

Get ranked

Your trial performance earns an official score. Prior association with a recognised cricket academy, state-affiliated cricket academy, or an ex-international cricketers academy will also be factored into your ranking.

05

Get shortlisted

Top performers move to the next selection stage. Every confirmation will be given by official email ID of the company.

06

Player pool

Become available for approved team selection.

07

Get selected

Earn your place in a SATAACK 50 squad. Categorisation shall be done on the basis of ranking.

08

Play SATAACK 50
