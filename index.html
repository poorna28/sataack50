The format

50

Fifty balls. Full impact.

A short, high-pressure format where every delivery matters. SATAACK 50 rewards quick thinking, brave decisions and players who can change a game immediately.

For batters

Intent matters. Every scoring option and every dot ball carries weight.

For bowlers

Accuracy, control and variation have to work from ball one.

For the game

Every run, decision and moment carries weight.

For fans

100 balls of total match action. Almost no dead time.
