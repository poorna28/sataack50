Which team identities are currently in place?

Hyderabad Heat, Bengaluru Voltage, Mumbai Surge and Delhi Drive are the first four established identities.

Are these four teams fully formed?

No. Their names and competitive personalities are in place. Crests, leadership, squads, kits and other details will be announced separately.

Will there be more teams?

Additional identities may be introduced according to the final Season 1 competition structure.

When will squads be announced?

Squad announcements will follow trials, evaluation, player-pool qualification and the official team-selection process.

Does entering the player pool guarantee a team?

No. It makes a player eligible for consideration but does not guarantee selection.

Can I apply to own or partner with a team?

Commercial, ownership and partnership enquiries should use the dedicated franchise or partnership pages.
