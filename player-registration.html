After registration

What happens
next?

A completed form begins the process. It does not complete selection.

  1. 01

    Application received

    Your profile and uploaded documents enter the registration review queue.

  2. 02

    Details verified

    Identity, contact information and eligibility may be checked.

  3. 03

    Trial opportunity

    Eligible applicants may receive city, date and reporting instructions when slots are available.

  4. 04

    Assessment begins

    Players are assessed through the published trial and selection process.
