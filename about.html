The league at a glance

A new competitive identity for tennis-ball cricket.

SATAACK 50 brings the accessibility, energy and improvisational character of tennis-ball cricket into an organised league environment.

50 Balls Per Innings | 10 Overs | 5 Balls Per Over | Men's & Women's Competitions | 6 Franchises Each | Indian + International Players | Age Group 18-30 | Season 1 - February 2027

It connects players discovered through trials with city-based team identities, structured competition, performance visibility and a growing audience.

The league is being designed to be quick to understand, exciting to watch and demanding to play.

The idea behind SATAACK 50

The cricket
already exists.
The stage
does not.

Across neighbourhood grounds, schools, colleges, academies, workplaces and box-cricket arenas, the players, competition and ability are already real.

Yet much of this cricket remains disconnected, unrecorded and difficult to discover. A strong performance can disappear after the match. A capable player can compete for years without a recognised profile, organised pathway or larger audience.

SATAACK 50 exists to connect that cricket to identity, opportunity and a stage.

Why SATAACK 50

Six reasons.
One league.

A competition designed around the realities of how tennis-ball cricket is played, discovered and followed.

01

A format built for impact

Fifty balls create immediate pressure. Every decision, effort and delivery carries visible consequence.

02

A pathway beyond the local ground

A defined journey connects player registration, trials, assessment, qualification and team consideration.

03

An identity for the player

Roles, performances, statistics, milestones and stories make a player easier to see, follow and remember.

04

Teams people can belong to

Cities, colours, characters and rivalries give players something to represent and audiences something to follow.

05

Cricket designed for today’s audience

A compact, direct format delivers momentum and meaningful action without losing competitive depth.

06

A larger ecosystem around the game

Coaches, organisers, venues, creators, technology partners, brands and cricket communities can all contribute.

Our mission

M

Give everyday cricket a visible competitive pathway.

Discover tennis-ball cricket talent, organise it into a credible competitive structure and give players a stage on which their ability, identity and stories can be recognised.

To discover talent, create opportunity and establish a credible competitive pathway for Men's and Women's tennis-ball cricket.

Our vision

V

Build India’s defining stage for tennis-ball cricket.

A future with visible players, trusted pathways, strong team identities, engaged communities and sustainable opportunities around the game.

To build a distinctive global platform for competitive tennis-ball cricket - combining grassroots opportunity, international participation, franchise sport, entertainment and professional sporting standards.
