Official participant classification

Participant does not
mean selected.

Every public profile carries its current official stage. Only a player selected by a team becomes a SATAACK 50 league player.

01

Registered

An official trial application has been submitted.

02

Confirmed

Requirements are complete and a trial place is confirmed.

03

Attended

The player has participated in the official trial.

04

Evaluated

The player’s trial performance has been assessed.

05

Shortlisted

The player has progressed to the next selection stage.

06

Player pool

The qualified player may be considered by teams.

Trial participation, public profile visibility, evaluation or shortlisting does not guarantee team selection.

the status means.

View all participant FAQs
Does appearing here mean I have been selected?

No. This is a directory of trial participants. Your current official stage is displayed clearly on your profile.

Why is my profile not visible?

Your registration may still be under verification, your trial place may not be confirmed, or public-profile consent may not be complete.

Will trial scores be public?

Only performance information approved under the official publication policy will be displayed. Private selector notes remain private.

Does being featured improve selection chances?

No. Editorial features and participant stories do not influence official evaluation or team selection.

How will I know if I am shortlisted?

Official updates will be sent through your participant account and registered contact details.
