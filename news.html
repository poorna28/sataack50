Season 1 development

SATAACK 50 moves closer to its opening season.

The first competitive identities are in place, the player pathway has been defined and preparations are progressing towards trials, qualification, team formation and the opening competition.

  • The competition will use a 50-ball format.
  • The league is built around tennis-ball cricket.
  • Players will enter through an organised trial and selection pathway.
  • Four initial team identities have been introduced.
